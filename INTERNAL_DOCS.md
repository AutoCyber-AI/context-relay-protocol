# CRP Internal Documentation Reference

Internal strategy, audit, and business documents have been moved to a
**private repository**: `CRP_internal_docs`.

These documents are intentionally excluded from the public CRP repository
to protect business strategy and intellectual property.

## Where to Find Internal Docs

| Category | Documents | Location |
|----------|-----------|----------|
| **Business & Strategy** | Business Action Plan, Financial Model, Product Catalog, Product Vision, Product Strategy, Trust & Value | `CRP_internal_docs/` (private) |
| **Security & IP** | Threat Analysis, Privacy Architecture, AI Governance Research | `CRP_internal_docs/` (private) |
| **Audits & Assessments** | Production Assessments (v1 & v2), Gap Audit, Deep Audit Gaps, Multi-Perspective Audit, Live Verification | `CRP_internal_docs/` (private) |
| **Task Tracking** | Master TODO, Known Issues, Gap Fix TODO | `CRP_internal_docs/` (private) |
| **SDK Internal** | Internal TODO, SDK Implementation Plan | `CRP_internal_docs/docs_internal/` (private) |
