APP 4 · Long-Context Document Generation

Long-Context Document Generation

A small local model loaded with only a fraction of its context ceiling cannot hold a long document in its head. CRP's continuation engine writes window by window, relays the document state forward, and stitches every window into one coherent deliverable. Watch it happen live, then check the acceptance gate that proves the document is real: length, structure, and near-zero repetition.
