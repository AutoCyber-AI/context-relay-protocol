# CRP Self-Hosting & Deployment

CRP Gateway and CRP Comply are available as a **managed cloud service** at
`gateway.crprotocol.io` and `comply.crprotocol.io`. You can also run them in
your own infrastructure.

## Managed cloud

The fastest path to production:

- **CRP Gateway** - `https://gateway.crprotocol.io`
- **CRP Comply** - `https://comply.crprotocol.io`

Sign up at the relevant endpoint; every plan includes the open SDK, HMAC audit
chain, and safety profiles.

## Self-hosted and white-label deployments

For organisations that need to run inside their own VPC, on-premises, or
embedded under their own brand, AutoCyber AI offers:

- Self-hosted Gateway + Comply containers
- Kubernetes sidecar deployment
- White-label / OEM licensing
- Air-gapped and data-residency configurations
- Signed DPAs, ISO 27001 audit packs, and 99.95% uptime SLAs

Detailed deployment variables, architecture diagrams, and operational runbooks
are provided under NDA to Enterprise and white-label customers.
