## What is a cookie?

A **cookie** is a small text file placed on your device by a website. We also refer here to
similar browser-storage mechanisms (localStorage, sessionStorage).

---

## What we use

crprotocol.io is a **documentation site with minimal tracking**. We use no advertising cookies
and no cross-site behavioural tracking.

### Strictly necessary

These cannot be switched off. They are required for the site to function.

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `__Secure-mkdocs_palette` | `localStorage` | Stores your chosen colour scheme (light / dark / system) | Persistent (cleared when you clear site data) |
| `__Secure-mkdocs_search` | `sessionStorage` | Caches search index for the current session | Session (cleared when you close the tab) |

### Analytics (aggregate, privacy-preserving)

If we enable server-side analytics (no client-side script, no cookies):

| Method | What we collect | Third party? |
|---|---|---|
| Server access logs | Anonymised page path, referrer (trimmed to domain), approximate country | No - our own server |

No cookies are set for analytics purposes.

### What we do NOT use

- :x: Google Analytics / Tag Manager
- :x: Meta Pixel / advertising pixels
- :x: Cross-site tracking cookies
- :x: Fingerprinting scripts
- :x: Session-recording tools (Hotjar, FullStory, etc.)

## How to control cookies

You can delete or block cookies at any time via your browser settings:

- **Chrome:** Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies
- **Firefox:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
- **Safari:** Preferences → Privacy
- **Edge:** Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Blocking strictly-necessary storage will break the dark/light theme toggle and search cache, but
the documentation content will still be accessible.
