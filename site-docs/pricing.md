## Which tier is right for me?

!!! tip "Solo developer or prototype"
    Start with **CRP Gateway Developer ($29/mo)** or **CRP Comply Free**. You get the full governance contract on every call with no seat-based pricing.

!!! tip "Small team shipping to production"
    **CRP Gateway Team ($199/mo)** + **CRP Comply Starter ($49/mo)** gives you multi-tenant routing, 5,000 compliant requests/mo, and evidence packs for security reviews.

!!! tip "Regulated enterprise or high-volume workload"
    **CRP Comply Scale ($499/mo)** or **Enterprise custom** for SSO, data residency, signed certificates, and 7-year audit retention. Add **CRP Scan Pro ($99/mo/org)** to catch governance gaps in CI.

!!! tip "Open source maintainer"
    **CRP Scan Public Repos** is free forever. Add one workflow step and every PR gets a governance check.
